Longtime College Football Announcer Tragically Died During Opening Weekend

August 30, 2026, 1:52pm EST 711 • By Arnab Mondal

Oregon State fans are remembering a voice.

College football lost a familiar voice over the weekend as longtime Oregon State Beavers announcer Darrell Aune died at 83.

Aune spent 29 years calling Oregon State sports. His voice became closely linked with Beavers football for generations of fans. His death came as the 2026 college football season began with several Week 0 games on Saturday.

Oregon State shared the news and remembered Aune for his long connection to the program.

“Remembering the Voice of the Beavers for 29 years, Darrell Aune. Our thoughts are with Darrell’s family and the many who knew and loved him,” Oregon State announced.

Aune gave Oregon State fans a way to follow the team during an era when televised college sports remained limited. For many listeners, his calls became part of their childhood and their memories of the Beavers.

“During my time at OSU Darrell was as engaged and supportive as one could be…having seen both the best of times and then the pains of recovery and growth he remained energetic, positive and professional bringing OSU sports to the loyal listeners-so grateful for him,” one fan wrote.

Another fan recalled growing up with Aune’s broadcasts.

“For every Corvallis kid from the 70s and 80s, Darrell Aune was the voice of our childhood. If you’re too young to have heard Darrell, this is what loving Oregon State University sounds like,” the fan wrote.

One former Oregon State fan also remembered Aune’s reaction after the Beavers upset Washington in Seattle in 1985.

“He might have been even happier than the coaches and players. A special person, and a great friend. I will miss him greatly,” the fan wrote.

Another fan said Aune’s voice offered a window into Oregon State sports when television coverage remained rare.

“As a kid in the 80s, it was very rare for Oregon State to have televised sports and the voice of Darrell Aune was our window to that world,” the fan wrote.

Aune leaves behind a lasting connection to Oregon State sports and the listeners who grew up hearing his voice.
