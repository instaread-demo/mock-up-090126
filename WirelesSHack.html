Types of Kodi Streaming Addons

Currently, there are three main types of streaming addons Torrent, Real Debrid, and Free Link addons.

The categories include movies and TV shows, sports, IPTV, and live tv.

Torrent addons pull in Torrent Links which is only recommended for advanced users.

Debrid addons pull in links from a paid Debrid services such as Real Debrid, All-Debrid, and Premiumize.

Free link addons pull in links that are freely available on the internet.

This page list addons that have the best free links unless otherwise listed.
