Austin Creed & Kofi signs with AEW

August 30, 2026 by M.A LeBlanc
  • The New Level, consisting of Kofi Kingston and Austin Creed, made their debut at AEW All In: London, where they joined forces with Swerve Strickland to enter the Trios Roulette Royale for the AEW World Trios Championship. Their surprise entry and victory marked a significant moment in AEW, as they became the first AEW wrestlers to win a title in their first appearance. The duo’s performance showcased their skills and teamwork, leading to a massive ovation from the crowd at Wembley Stadium. The New Level’s victory at All In: London is a testament to their talent and the fresh start they have achieved in AEW.
