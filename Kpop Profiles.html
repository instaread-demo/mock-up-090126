Kilala Profile and Facts

Kilala (키라라) is a singer and dancer under INNIT Entertainment. She is a member of the girl group OURBIRTHDAY.

Stage Name: Kilala (키라라)
Birth Name: Takashima Kirara (高島きらら)
Birthday: December 17, 2010
Zodiac Sign: Sagittarius
Chinese Zodiac Sign: Tiger
Height: 160 cm (5’2″)
Weight: N/A
Blood Type: N/A
MBTI Type: ENFP
Nationality: Japanese
Representative Color: Coral
Representative Emoticon: 🍓 Strawberry
WHIP! Avatar: Kirakle (키라클레)

Kilala Facts:
Life & Background:
– She was born in Sapporo, Japan.
– Her nicknames are Lala, Lalaki, Kkyaeng and Pink Princess.
– She found out about TWICE on social media when she was in elementary school, which inspired her to audition at JYP Entertainment as she was impressed by their charisma and teamwork.
– She was a trainee under JYP Entertainment before moving to INNIT Entertainment.
– She is close with members of the boy group CIIU under JYP China.
– She was ranked 3rd in Women’s Classical Ballet at the 2021 Japan Ballet Competition.
– At the dorms, she shares a four-person room with members Chorok, Hyewon, and Achiraya.

Hobbies & Interests:
– Her hobbies are shopping, chatting with people, and watching Anime.
– Her special talents are playing the piano and ballet.
– She likes to use and check out new makeup products.
– She loves everything about the color pink and she sometimes gets called the Pink Maknae.

Personality & Traits:
– She is recognized for her versatility in both dancing and having a pretty voice with powerful vocals.
– She acts as the Pest Control for her members, taking care of catching all the bugs.
– Kilala says since she’s still in her growth phase, she is often the one who gets hungriest the most.
– Hyejin says that Kilala looks like a raccoon with her big round eyes and egg-shaped face, while Kilala herself thinks she looks more like a rabbit.
– Baby and Achiraya says that Kilala likes compliments and that she can accomplish any tasks if you shower her with praises.
– Her members gave her the nickname “MacGyver,” because she is so handy with tools.
– Hyewon mentions that Kilala is put together all the member’s bunk beds with just a wrench.
– Her members say that Kilala would survive longest in a zombie apocalypse since she can likely build defenses.
– She credits her mother for helping her chase her dreams of becoming a K-pop idol.
– Her debut message to fans: “I’m so happy to debut and perform with the members I’ve always dreamed being with. Please continue to support and show lots of love for OURBIRTHDAY!”

Other Facts:
– She was revealed as a member of OURBIRTHDAY on July 25, 2026.
– She was a part of the OURBIRTHDAY pre-debut sub-unit named “Side B” with Chorok, Hyewon and U, which debuted on July 29, 2026 with the single “HUNGRY”.
– She made her official debut on August 19, 2026, with OURBIRTHDAY with the single “SQUEEZY”.
– She is the first foreign-born member born in the 2010’s to debut in K-pop.
