FAQs

What is the most expensive car in NFS Unbound?

If you’ve got the cash to splash, the Bugatti Chiron Sport holds the crown as the most expensive car in NFS Unbound, setting you back a cool $3,665,000. For that mountain of cash, you get a beast that hits a top speed of 420 km/h and launches from 0 to 97 km/h in a blistering 2.3 seconds.

What is the best car to buy in NFS Unbound?

When it comes to the absolute best car in NFS Unbound, the Koenigsegg Regera is in a class of its own. Boasting a staggering top speed of over 412 km/h and mind-bending acceleration, it’s the king of the streets. It’ll cost you a hefty $1,500,000, but for serious racers looking for unparalleled performance and razor-sharp handling, the Regera is worth every penny. It’s the ultimate weapon for dominating competitive races and leaving cops in the dust.

What much is the Koenigsegg Regera in NFS Unbound?

The Koenigsegg Regera will set you back $1,500,000 in NFS Unbound. It’s a steep price, but as the game’s top performer, it’s an investment in victory.

What is the fastest car in NFS Unbound?

Hands down, the Koenigsegg Regera is the fastest car in NFS Unbound. This machine is a marvel of engineering; with no gears to shift, it delivers one continuous, brutal wave of acceleration all the way up to its top speed of 256 mph (412 km/h).

What is the price of Bugatti Chiron Sport in NFS Unbound?

Get ready to open your wallet wide. The Bugatti Chiron Sport in NFS Unbound costs a staggering $3,665,000. It’s the most expensive ride in the game, boasting a top speed of 420 km/h and a 0-97 km/h time of just 2.3 seconds.

What is the cheapest car in Need for Speed Unbound?

Looking to get started without breaking the bank? The Volkswagen Beetle 1963 is the cheapest car in Need for Speed Unbound, coming in at only $18,000. Don’t let the low price fool you—this classic Bug is a fantastic starting point for any street racer on a budget.

How many cars are in NFS Unbound?

NFS Unbound features a massive roster of 143 cars from 33 different manufacturers. The lineup ranges from the budget-friendly Volkswagen Beetle to the hyper-expensive Bugatti Chiron Sport.
