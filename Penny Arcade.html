How to Find Me @ PAX!

By Gabe

Instaread

PAX West is coming up this week and I will of course be there PAXING it up with everyone else. Here is a look at my schedule and the best times to find me at the show.

 

 

Friday

I will be on stage doing the Penny Arcade Make a Strip panel in the Main Theatre at 12PM.

This is a Q&A with Jerry and I, a live comic drawing and a fundraiser for Child’s Play all wrapped into one incredible panel!

Saturday

I will once again be in the main theatre, this time for Acquisitions Incorporated at 7:00pm 

I was sad I had to miss the last game at PAX East but it is not the first time my human body has failed me and I am sure it will not be the last. Acquisitions Inc. is easily the most authentic of the Live Play TTRPG games if only because it has a player (ME!) who misses some games and when he does show up, doesn’t really know the rules. 


Sunday

There has apparently been a resurgence in requests for a designated time and place to say hi to Jerry and I and maybe get something signed so we are bringing it back! Come see us for the Penny Arcade Meet and Greet in the Autograph Area at 12:30pm. We are happy to sign anything that does not burn, bite or sting! 

 

I will also be attending the community pin trading events when I can. If you really want to find me, keep an eye on my Bluesky account over the weekend as I will use that to announce if I am going to post up someplace to trade pins or just hang out. 

 


See you nerds at PAX!

 


-Gabe Out
