LEGO Seasonal Christmas 2026 Sets Officially Announced

August 31, 2026 / Allen "Tormentalous" Tran

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 4:07
Instaread

LEGO has officially revealed three new LEGO Seasonal Christmas 2026 sets that will be releasing in October. These festive sets includes one from my favorite Christmas movie, Elf, which is a very unexpected license to get. The other sets feature a functional Santa calendar and an up-scaled minifigure of Mrs. Claus to pair up with Santa from last year.

Buddy the Elf (40865) – 713 pieces/$39.99

We know him! Earn a spot on Santa’s nice list with this detailed LEGO® Buddy the Elf (40865) building set. Fans of the classic Elf movie can plan a whole day of building snow angels, going ice skating and showing their love for Buddy as they assemble their own detailed decoration. Buddy the Elf is ready for delivery day, standing proudly atop his ice-shaped base at the North Pole along with friends and other details from the movie. Can you spot the arctic puffin, candy canes and maple syrup bottle? The model building kit makes a whimsical toy that will brighten up any shelf, tabletop, or Christmas display scene. This set makes a fun gift for kids aged 12 and over – or anyone who loves the Elf movie. Team up with friends and family with Build Together mode in the LEGO Builder app, where everyone has fun constructing parts of the set using their own devices. This set contains 713 pieces.

Santa’s Holiday Countdown (40874) – 873 pieces/$59.99

‘Tis the season to get ready for the big day with the LEGO® Santa’s Holiday Countdown (40874) décor building set for builders aged 10 and over. This enchanting Christmas toy lets families create a fun display piece with a perpetual calendar, pink-cheeked Santa Claus and more. Bring the gift of magic to Christmas and create jolly ol’ St. Nick as he prepares to slide down the chimney on a snowy roof with his sack of gifts beside him. Will he make it in time? With each new day, use the knobs to turn the countdown blocks on the chimney and get that much closer to Christmas. The Santa Claus décor kit makes a great wintertime family activity gift for older girls, boys and friends. Display it on a mantlepiece or underneath the tree and create a family tradition by rebuilding it every year. Plus, kids can use the LEGO Builder app for an easy, intuitive adventure that lets them zoom and rotate in 3D, save and track their progress. This set contains 873 pieces.

Up-Scaled Mrs. Claus Minifigure (40875) – 904 pieces/$59.99

Embrace the Christmas spirit the LEGO® way! Add festive cheer to any seasonal décor with the Up-Scaled Mrs. Claus Minifigure (40875), a delightful set for kids aged 10 and over and families who love building together. This super-sized version of Santa’s leading lady transforms the classic LEGO minifigure into an up-scaled, detailed Christmas figure that comes to life with a posable head, arms and hands. Mrs. Claus brings magic and warmth, and with her happy smile, makes the perfect display piece either alone or as an addition to desk or tabletop seasonal décor. The kit makes a great Christmas or Advent gift for kids, girls and boys. Create a family tradition by rebuilding Mrs. Claus every year at the start of the season. Plus, family and friends can team up together with Build Together mode in the LEGO Builder app, where everyone has fun constructing parts of the set using their own devices. This set contains 904 pieces.
