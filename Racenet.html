'You can only run so fast for 1000m': Sears' gun is Ready for Concorde

The Tony and Maddysen Sears-trained flying machine Steady Ready. Picture: Melanie Ward / Trackside Photography
By Ben Dorries

Tony Sears is sending nippy Queensland sprinter Steady Ready into the big league in Sydney with a simple philosophy in mind.

"You can only run so fast for 1000m and he can run very fast over that distance," Sears said.

"He will give them something to catch, that's for sure."

Steady Ready is no slouch, having won eight races including a Listed event and banking more than $1.3m for connections.

But the now seven-year-old has never competed in a Group race.

Sears, who trains with daughter Maddysen, knows he is throwing his sprinter into the deep end in the $1 million Group 2 Concorde Stakes (1000m) at Randwick on Saturday.

However, the Gold Coast trainer sees no reason not to launch his speedy galloper into the lion's den of Sydney racing in a hit-and-run mission.

Steady Ready was last seen winning a $200,000 race over 1000m on the Sunshine Coast at the end of the winter carnival and has warmed up for his Sydney assignment by winning a pair of shortcourse trials.

"When he won that race on the Sunshine Coast, we sat down and had a good think about what to do with him," Sears said.

• Ka Ying Rising impresses ahead of race return

"There wasn't much in Queensland for him.

"There was only a couple of 1000m races around that would suit him and the one in Sydney on Saturday was one of them.

"We were thinking it might not be quite so good a field, coming up to The Everest we thought some horses might go other ways, but it's as strong as anything.

"I didn't think it would be quite as strong a race as it is.

"But it is what it is, you can't do much about it.

"He's a terrific 1000m horse and he's going really well, so why not give him a chance?"

Tony and Maddysen Sears. Picture: Trackside Photography.

• ‘Why not, what's to lose?' Pericles set for surprise Group 1 kick-off

In early betting markets, Steady Ready is rated a $26 chance to spring an upset in the Concorde in which he will be ridden by Tommy Berry.

"Look, we think this horse is going as good as he has ever gone, probably better," Sears said.

"He is pretty well weighted I think in this race, with 56(kg).

"After this race, he will come back, we haven't really thought of anything after that.

"He is a Magic Millions horse and is a good horse in the Queensland summer when some of those better races come around."

• ‘It's just devastating': Champion pacer euthanised

Five of Steady Ready's eight career wins have come over the specialist 1000m distance, with another of his wins coming over the 900m scamper.

While Steady Ready is unlikely to be seen again in Sydney this spring, the Sears' stable star Yellow Brick will be Sydney-bound for The Everest day in October.

Yellow Brick will launch a first-up attack on the $2 million Group 2 Sydney Stakes (1200m) at Randwick on Everest day.

It was the race won last year by another Queenslander, Rothfire, with help from one of the best rides you will ever see from Hong Kong-based superstar Zac Purton.
