Apple Unveils Cutting-Edge Mac Studio and Updated Mac Mini

On Tuesday, August 25, Apple unveiled two highly anticipated devices: the Mac Studio and the Mac mini. Here’s everything you need to know.

Summary

  • M5 Max Mac Studio

  • M5 Ultra Mac Studio

  • Mac Studio Form Factor and Features

  • Mac Studio and AI

  • Mac mini M6

  • Mac mini M5 Pro

  • Design of the Mac mini

  • Prices and Availability

August 25 marks a significant day for Cupertino’s tech giant. Officially launching the new generation of Mac mini and Mac Studio, following Mark Gurman’s unofficial announcement this morning regarding the Mac mini. From new chips to performance and pricing, here’s all you need to know about these products.

Mac Studio

M5 Max Mac Studio

Apple describes the Mac Studio as “the ultimate desktop computer for professionals.” Equipped with the M5 Max chip, an enhanced version of the previous M4 Max launched this year with the MacBook Pro, it boasts the following features, according to Apple’s official site:

  • 18-core CPU

  • Up to 40-core GPU

  • Up to 128 GB of unified memory

  • Up to 614 GB/s of memory bandwidth

  • 16-core Neural Engine

Additionally, the M5 Max Mac Studio, designed both for creative and development professionals, offers graphic performance up to 50% better than the previous generation and “onboard AI computing capabilities.” With this product, you can edit “stacks of high-resolution RAW images” or even generate “real-time 3D graphic animations.”

Looking at its performance, Apple has shared some benchmark figures. For instance, with the M5 Max Mac Studio, the time to the first token in LM Studio is 10.7 times faster than with the M1 Max and 3.9 times faster than with the M4 Max.

The Mac Studio starts with 36 GB of memory, which can be expanded up to 128 GB.

M5 Ultra Mac Studio

The M5 Ultra Mac Studio “features the most powerful GPU ever integrated into an Apple chip and the highest number of CPU cores ever seen on a Mac,” the company announces. It combines two M5 Max chips into a single system, making it the highest-performing for artificial intelligence.

This model is equipped with a processor up to 36 cores, including 12 super cores and 24 high-performance cores, and a GPU that can have up to 80 cores.

It comes with 96 GB of RAM and can be configured up to 512 GB. The memory bandwidth reaches up to 1.2 TB/s, as noted by 9to5Mac.

Apple also highlights its performance, for example, processing LLM prompts up to 9.8 times faster in LM Studio with the M5 Ultra Mac Studio than with the M1 Ultra, and up to four times faster than with the M3 Ultra.

Form Factor and Other Features of the Mac Studio

Apple packs all these new features into a square casing less than 20 centimeters on each side. Network connectivity advances with the introduction of Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6, powered by the new N1 chip. Regarding display, the machine can now manage up to eight external displays simultaneously, or four Studio Display XDRs in 5K at 120 Hz.

It includes 12 ports and supports Thunderbolt 5 and USB‑C. It features an SDXC card reader, 2 USB‑A ports, an HDMI, an Ethernet, and a headphone jack.

Mac Studio and AI

When it comes to artificial intelligence, the Mac Studio is designed to locally handle the most demanding AI workloads, without consistently relying on the cloud. Developers can run large language models like Gemma, Kimi, or Mistral directly on the device, through apps like LM Studio Bionic, or generate images with Stable Diffusion or FLUX in Draw Things. This on-device approach enhances responsiveness while maintaining control over sensitive data, a privacy issue that Apple emphasizes with Apple Intelligence and Private Cloud Compute, designed to never expose personal information.

Mac Mini

Mac mini M6

This time, Apple opts for the new M6 chip for the base configuration, which is Apple’s first 2nm processor. It features a 12-core processor, specifically 2 ultra-high-performance cores, 4 performance cores, and 6 low-power cores.

You will also benefit from a 12-core GPU. For the first time, Apple integrates its neural accelerators, quadrupling its AI performance.

It is offered with 16 GB of unified memory, expandable up to 32 GB. Its bandwidth is 170 GB/s.

Mac mini M5 Pro

The high-end Mac mini is equipped with the M5 Pro chip. It includes a processor with up to 18 cores and a graphics processor with up to 20 cores. Each core contains neural accelerators. It is available with 64 GB of memory and a bandwidth of 307 GB/s.

Design of the Mac Mini

The Mac mini is offered in a square format measuring 12.7 cm on each side. It has 2 USB-C ports, a headphone jack, 2.5 Gigabit Ethernet, 3 Thunderbolt ports, and an HDMI.

Increased Pricing and Availability of the New Macs

All these products are available for pre-order starting today. However, the prices have increased. The Mac mini is available starting at $1,049, and the Mac Studio starts at $2,999.
