Meet the TECNO SPARK Go 3 Pro: Budget Phone, Rugged Armor

Who said budget phones can't be tough?

By: Sly Juan
August 29, 2026

TECNO has officially launched the SPARK Go 3 Pro in India, bringing IP69-rated durability, a 120Hz display, and a 5,200mAh battery to the entry-level smartphone segment.

The phone is designed for demanding everyday environments, including outdoor work, delivery operations, agricultural settings, and crowded commutes. Its durability is also a key part of TECNO’s “Desh Jaisa Dumdaar” campaign.

TECNO SPARK Go 3 Pro specifications

  • Display: 6.78-inch, 120Hz
  • Durability: IP69
  • RAM: 4GB
  • Storage: 64GB / 128GB
  • Battery: 5,200mAh
  • Charging: 15W
  • Audio: Dual speakers, DTS
  • Noise cancellation: AI Voiceprint Noise Cancellation
  • Connectivity: IR blaster

The IP69 rating provides protection against dust and high-pressure water jets, while the device is also designed to withstand accidental drops and physical impact.

TECNO also highlights its AI Voiceprint Noise Cancellation technology, which is designed to reduce background noise during calls in loud environments. The phone includes an IR blaster for controlling compatible home appliances, along with dual speakers and DTS support.

Price and availability

The TECNO SPARK Go 3 Pro will go on sale in India starting September 4, 2026, through offline retail stores as well as Amazon and Flipkart.

At its price point, the SPARK Go 3 Pro stands out by putting durability at the center of the package, bringing an IP69 rating to a segment where rugged protection is far less common.
